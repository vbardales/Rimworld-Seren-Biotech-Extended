# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Twelve body mask genes (`SerenOriginalMask_0` to `SerenOriginalMask_11`) ported from the original Seren mod to Seren, from Stardust [Biotech Fork].
- Horn genes (`SerenOriginalHorns_0` to `SerenOriginalHorns_7`) and twelve accent colour genes (`SerenAccent_*`) that tint the horns and body masks; a small assembly (`SerenBiotechExtended.dll`) does the tinting.
- Mod option to choose the face set of Seren when both Facial Animation frameworks are active: Nals Experimentals (default) or Vanilla Textures Expanded. A restart is needed after changing it. A shortcut button to the same options is hidden by default and can be revealed with RIMMSQOL or a similar mod.
- Seren player pawn kinds of the original scenarios (tribal, medieval, viking, pirate, classical, ronin, edo).
- Seren longevity gene (`OL_SerenLongevity`, biological ageing slowed to 0.214 from age 20): new Seren pawns live about three hundred years like the original race, instead of ageing like a Human.
- The eight Seren creepjoiner forms of the original mod (Anomaly) and the space-born gene (`OL_SpaceBorn`).
